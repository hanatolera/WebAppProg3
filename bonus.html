My Superhero Duo

Heroic Tiger and Hibiscus

Heroic Tiger is a brave and powerful superhero with the strength and speed of a tiger. Hibiscus is the loyal sidekick who uses the power of plants to help Heroic Tiger protect the city.

Foes

  • Time Travelor

    • Minor Power: Can change the color of objects.
